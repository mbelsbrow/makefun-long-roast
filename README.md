One satirical sentence roasting the lawmakers who would regulate artificial intelligence but cannot convert a file to PDF: the chairman of the committee that will regulate AI emails his staff a photo of his monitor, because the button marked Export to PDF looked, in his words, "a bit technical."

## Entry

> The chairman of the committee that will regulate artificial intelligence emailed his staff a photo of his monitor this morning, because the button marked Export to PDF looked, in his words, 'a bit technical.'

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, one line, 208 characters). Node 22+, standard library only, no network access, deterministic.
